MyPlant

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

MyPlant works without an account, and then your plants, care history, journal and photos stay on your device. This policy explains what leaves your device, and when.

Without an account

  • Everything stays on your device. Plants, care history, journal entries, photos and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Weather (optional). If you allow location or pick a city, the app rounds the position to about 1 km and sends only that, through our server, to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) to get a forecast, and to Apple to look up the city name. Our server passes the rounded position on without anything that identifies you, and briefly keeps the forecast for that spot to answer the next request for it. It never stores your position with your account.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't use push servers.

With an account

An account backs up and syncs your plants, and is needed for the AI features unless you use your own API key. We keep:

  • your email address and a hashed password, to sign you in;
  • with Sign in with Apple: instead of a password, the email address Apple shares (or a private relay address, if you choose Hide My Email) and an anonymous ID from Apple for this app. We don't ask Apple for your name;
  • your plants, care history, journal entries, AI check-ups and follow-up chats, and photos, to sync them across your devices;
  • a record of each AI use (when, which feature, which model, how many drops 💧 it cost and how much text the model processed), to count your daily drops and keep track of what the service costs us.

Before photos are stored or sent anywhere, the app resizes them and re-encodes them, which removes metadata such as where they were taken.

AI features

MyPlant sends nothing to an AI provider until you allow it in the app, which asks the first time you use an AI feature. You can stop at any time in Settings → AI → Sharing with AI; the app then asks again before sending anything.

When you identify a plant, check its health (including follow-up questions) or ask for care suggestions, the app sends:

  • the plant photos you choose;
  • what you type or select: a plant's name, your notes and questions, the symptoms you tick, and the earlier messages in that check-up's conversation;
  • your plant's care details: its species, whether it lives indoors, on a balcony or outdoors, whether it's near a window, how often it's watered, and how many days since it was last watered and fed;
  • your app language.

We don't include your name, email address, plant nicknames or location. This goes through our server to one of these AI providers:

  • Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen models), servers in mainland China. Used for identification and care suggestions, and for check-ups unless you pick Claude. In mainland China it is the only provider.
  • Anthropic (Claude models), servers in the United States. Used for check-ups and follow-ups when you pick Claude, outside mainland China.

Our server uses your account only to count your daily drops and doesn't pass your account details to the AI provider. The providers process the request to return an answer. Under their terms for API customers they don't use it to train their models, and they may keep it for a limited time (for example, to monitor abuse). Our server doesn't keep the photos or answers. Only what you save, such as a diagnosis in a plant's history, is stored with your plants.

With your own API key

If you add your own key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Alibaba Cloud (Settings → Your Own AI Keys), the key is stored only in your iPhone's Keychain and is never sent to us. Remove it there at any time; if you delete the app, the key is cleared the next time MyPlant is installed and opened. AI requests then go straight from your iPhone to that provider, not through our server, under your own agreement with them. We don't see these requests or their answers, except what you save to your plants.

Reference photos

To help you compare a plant with the species it might be, the app shows openly licensed photos of that species. It asks our server for them by the species' scientific name only. Our server fetches the photos from Wikimedia Commons, keeps copies and shows each photographer and licence. No information about you is sent to Wikimedia, and your device never contacts it for these photos.

Ads (free plan only)

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on Today and My Plants, a full-screen ad after you add a plant (from your second plant on, at most once every 10 minutes), and rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for extra drops 💧. Lifetime, Plus and Pro have no ads.

To show ads and measure them, Google's ad SDK in the app collects, according to Google: a device identifier limited to this app or developer, approximate location derived from your IP address, the ads you see and how you interact with them, and crash and performance data. Google describes how it uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • No tracking. MyPlant doesn't ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the ad SDK doesn't get your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and the ads aren't based on following you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Your consent in the UK and EEA. Before any ad, the app shows Google's consent form where the law requires it. Change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Rewarded ads. When you watch one, the app gives Google your MyPlant account ID (a random code, not your email address), so that Google can tell our server which account earned the drops.
  • We never give Google or any advertiser your plants, photos, journal, AI conversations or email address.

Purchases

Apple handles payments; we never see your card or payment details. When you buy a plan while signed in, the app sends us Apple's signed record of the purchase, and Apple sends us updates such as renewals, expiries and refunds. With your account we keep the purchase's transaction ID, the product and plan, whether it was a test (sandbox) purchase, and when the plan ends, so that your plan works on all your devices. Without an account, the app checks your purchases with Apple on your device only.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage, sign-in and server functions, on servers in Seoul, South Korea. It also sends account emails.
  • Alibaba Cloud and Anthropic (and OpenAI, only with your own key), as described above.
  • Google (AdMob), for ads on the free plan, as described above.
  • MET Norway, for weather forecasts (through our server); Apple, for city names, Sign in with Apple and payments.
  • Wikimedia Commons, the source of the reference photos; only our server contacts it, with nothing but a species name.

We don't sell your data and don't track you across apps or websites. Apart from Google's ad SDK on the free plan, the app has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Short-lived server logs, which contain no photos, are deleted automatically after a few days.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This deletes your account, your synced data and your photos from our server right away. The plants on your iPhone stay until you delete them or the app. If you used Sign in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm first, and we then revoke MyPlant's access to your Apple ID with Apple. You can also stop using your Apple ID with MyPlant at any time in the iPhone Settings app (your name → Sign in with Apple). You can also contact us (see below) to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to how we use it. We use your data to provide the features you ask for, and to prevent abuse (such as through usage limits). To exercise these rights, contact us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

MyPlant isn't directed at children under 13 (under 14 in mainland China), and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can reach only its own data on our server.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. We'll tell you in the app before any change to how your data is shared.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at GitHub

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

不注册账号也能使用 MyPlant。不注册时，你的植物、照护记录、日记和照片都只保存在你的设备上。本政策说明哪些数据会离开你的设备，以及在什么情况下离开。

不注册账号时

  • 所有数据都留在你的设备上。植物、照护记录、日记、照片和设置只保存在你的 iPhone 上。
  • 天气（可选）。如果你允许定位或选择了城市，App 会把位置精确度降到约 1 公里，只把这个位置经由我们的服务器发送给挪威气象研究所（MET Norway）获取天气预报，并发送给 Apple 查询城市名称。我们的服务器转发这个大致位置时不附带任何能识别你的信息，并会把该地点的预报短暂保留，用来回应之后对同一地点的请求。它不会把你的位置和你的账号存在一起。
  • 提醒在你的设备上本地安排，我们不使用推送服务器。

注册账号后

注册账号后，你的植物会备份和同步。使用 AI 功能也需要账号（使用自己的 API Key 时除外）。我们会保存：

  • 你的邮箱地址和经过哈希处理的密码，用于登录；
  • 如果通过 Apple 登录：不设密码，我们保存 Apple 提供的邮箱地址（如果你选择「隐藏邮件地址」，则是 Apple 的中转地址），以及 Apple 为本 App 生成的匿名 ID。我们不会向 Apple 获取你的姓名；
  • 你的植物、照护记录、日记、AI 诊断结果和追问对话，以及照片，用于在你的设备之间同步；
  • 每次使用 AI 的记录（时间、功能、模型、花费的水滴 💧 数，以及模型处理的文字量），用于计算你每天的水滴额度，并核算服务成本。

照片在保存或发送之前，App 会先缩小尺寸并重新编码，这一步会去除拍摄地点等元数据。

AI 功能

在你于 App 内允许之前，MyPlant 不会向任何 AI 服务商发送内容。第一次使用 AI 功能时，App 会先询问你。你可以随时在「设置 → AI → 与 AI 共享」中停止共享，之后 App 在发送任何内容前都会再次询问你。

当你识别植物、做健康检查（包括追问）或获取照护建议时，App 会发送：

  • 你选择的植物照片；
  • 你输入或勾选的内容：植物名称、你的备注和问题、勾选的症状，以及这次健康检查中之前的对话；
  • 植物的照护信息：品种、放在室内、阳台还是室外、是否靠窗、多久浇一次水，以及距离上次浇水和施肥过了几天；
  • App 的语言。

我们不会附上你的名字、邮箱地址、植物昵称或位置。这些内容经由我们的服务器，发送给以下 AI 服务商之一：

  • 阿里云百炼（通义千问模型），服务器位于中国大陆。用于植物识别和照护建议；做健康检查时，如果你没有选择 Claude，也由它处理。在中国大陆，只使用这一家服务商。
  • Anthropic（Claude 模型），服务器位于美国。在中国大陆以外，你为健康检查和追问选择 Claude 时使用。

我们的服务器只用你的账号计算每天的水滴额度，不会把账号信息传给 AI 服务商。服务商处理请求，然后返回结果。根据它们面向 API 客户的条款，它们不会用这些数据训练模型，但可能会保存一段有限的时间（例如用于防止滥用）。我们的服务器不保存这些照片和回答。只有你保存下来的内容（例如植物历史中的诊断结果）会和你的植物数据一起存储。

使用自己的 API Key 时

如果你添加了自己在 Anthropic、OpenAI 或阿里云的 Key（「设置 → 自带 AI Key」），这个 Key 只保存在你 iPhone 的钥匙串里，不会发送给我们。你可以随时在那里移除它；如果你删除了 App，下次重新安装并打开 MyPlant 时，这个 Key 会被清除。之后的 AI 请求会从你的 iPhone 直接发给对应的服务商，不经过我们的服务器，适用你与该服务商之间的协议。我们看不到这些请求和回答，除非你把结果保存到植物记录中。

参考照片

为了方便你把植物和可能的品种对照，App 会显示该品种的开放授权照片。App 只用品种的学名向我们的服务器请求照片。我们的服务器从维基共享资源（Wikimedia Commons）获取照片，保存副本，并标明每张照片的作者和授权方式。我们不会把任何关于你的信息发送给维基媒体，你的设备也不会为这些照片直接访问它。

广告（仅限免费版）

免费版会显示来自 Google AdMob 的广告：「今天」和「我的植物」页面底部的横幅；添加植物后的全屏广告（从第二株植物开始，最多每 10 分钟一次）；以及你可以自愿观看、换取额外水滴 💧 的激励广告。终身版、Plus 和 Pro 没有广告。

为了展示和衡量广告，App 内的 Google 广告 SDK 会收集（据 Google 说明）：仅限于本 App 或本开发者范围内的设备标识符、根据 IP 地址推断的大致位置、你看到的广告以及与广告的互动，以及崩溃和性能数据。Google 如何使用这些数据，请见 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

  • 不追踪你。MyPlant 不会请求追踪权限（App 跟踪透明度），因此广告 SDK 拿不到你设备的广告标识符（IDFA），广告也不会基于你在其他公司的 App 和网站上的行为。
  • 英国和欧洲经济区的同意。在法律有要求的地区，App 会在显示任何广告之前先弹出 Google 的同意表单。你可以随时在「设置 → 关于 → 广告隐私选项」中更改选择。
  • 激励广告。当你观看激励广告时，App 会把你的 MyPlant 账号 ID（一串随机代码，不是你的邮箱）提供给 Google，以便 Google 告诉我们的服务器是哪个账号获得了水滴。
  • 我们绝不会把你的植物、照片、日记、AI 对话或邮箱地址提供给 Google 或任何广告商。

购买

付款由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡或任何付款信息。当你在登录状态下购买方案时，App 会把 Apple 签名的购买记录发送给我们，Apple 也会把续订、到期和退款等更新发送给我们。我们会把这笔购买的交易 ID、商品和方案、是否为测试（沙盒）购买，以及方案的到期时间与你的账号一起保存，让你的方案在你所有设备上都能使用。不登录时，App 只在你的设备上向 Apple 核对购买记录。

谁会处理你的数据

  • Supabase：托管我们的数据库、文件存储、登录服务和服务器函数，服务器位于韩国首尔，也负责发送账号相关的邮件。
  • 阿里云和 Anthropic（以及仅在你使用自己的 Key 时的 OpenAI）：见上文。
  • Google（AdMob）：免费版的广告，见上文。
  • MET Norway：天气预报（经由我们的服务器）；Apple：城市名称、通过 Apple 登录和付款。
  • 维基共享资源（Wikimedia Commons）：参考照片的来源。只有我们的服务器会访问它，而且只发送品种名称。

我们不出售你的数据，也不跨 App 或网站追踪你。除了免费版中的 Google 广告 SDK，App 没有任何广告或统计分析 SDK。

保存期限

账号数据保存到你删除账号为止。服务器的短期日志不包含照片，几天后会自动删除。

删除你的数据

在 App 中前往「设置 → 账号 → 删除账号」。删除后，我们会立即删除你的账号、服务器上的同步数据和照片。你 iPhone 上的植物会保留，直到你自己删除它们或删除 App。如果你是通过 Apple 登录的，Apple 会先请你确认，随后我们会通过 Apple 撤销 MyPlant 对你 Apple ID 的授权。你也可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置」App 中（点按你的名字 →「通过 Apple 登录」）停止在 MyPlant 中使用你的 Apple ID。你也可以联系我们（见下方），要求获取、更正或删除你的数据。

你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正、删除或导出你的数据，或者反对我们使用这些数据的方式。我们使用你的数据，是为了提供你要求的功能，以及防止滥用（例如每日额度）。如需行使这些权利，请联系我们。你也可以向当地的数据保护机构投诉。

儿童

MyPlant 不面向 14 岁以下的儿童（中国大陆以外地区为 13 岁以下），我们不会在知情的情况下收集他们的数据。

安全

数据在传输过程中经过加密（HTTPS）。每个账号只能访问我们服务器上属于自己的数据。

变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会更新上方的日期。在改变数据的共享方式之前，我们会先在 App 内告知你。
